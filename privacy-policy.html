Privacy Policy for Ludo Reimagined

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Ludo Reimagined is a board game you play on your own device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. In short: the app has no accounts, no servers of its own, and collects nothing about you directly. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the game free.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no registration. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it has no way to send such information anywhere. The "Guest Player" name shown on the home screen is a fixed label in the app, not a profile.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your preferences between sessions:

  • Your settings — sound on or off, and the turn timer length
  • Your in-game coin balance

This never leaves your device, is not readable by us or anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To do this, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares: device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID; your IP address, which is used to estimate your approximate location; app interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views; and diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage. This is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We do not receive any of it ourselves — it is collected and handled by Google, and is encrypted in transit.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalised advertising takes place, using Google's consent management platform. You can decline, and you can change your choice later. If consent is declined or cannot be established, the app does not request ads at all.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On Android: Settings → Google → Ads. On iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Opt out of personalised ads using the same settings, or at adssettings.google.com.
  • Delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 16 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to delete; if you have concerns about advertising shown on a shared device, the advertising ID controls linked above apply.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app currently includes no analytics and no crash reporting. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share any information about you, because we do not collect any. Advertising data is handled by Google under its own policies, linked above.
